DBbun / DE2Sim

Digital Engineering to Simulation — readable-to-runnable pipeline

DE2Sim converts standards-based digital-engineering artifacts (CAD-export geometry, a SysMLv2 subset, parametric data) into an Authoritative Source of Truth (ASOT), AI-assisted behavior proposals reviewed and approved by a human, and executable low- and high-fidelity simulation output.

Originally developed for the Army Training Verse Prize Challenge II ("Intelligent Simulation Pipeline").
